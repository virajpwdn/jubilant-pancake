## Overview

# 🧠 PersonaAI

**PersonaAI** is an AI-driven platform that allows users to interact with the personas of highly successful individuals to seek guidance, insights, and advice on their goals and plans.

---

## 🎯 Project Vision

The core idea behind PersonaAI is to make wisdom more accessible. Instead of passively consuming talks, podcasts, or interviews, users can actively converse with AI personas modeled after successful people and receive contextual, personalized guidance.

---

## 🔍 How It Works

![Video Thumbnail](/sysdesign/persona-hld.png)

- Each persona is trained using **publicly available content**, including:
  - Talks and presentations
  - TED Talks
  - Podcasts and interviews
  - Articles and written material
- The system analyzes communication style, reasoning patterns, and domain expertise.
- Users can ask questions related to:
  - Career planning
  - Personal growth
  - Decision-making
  - Business and life strategy

---

## ⚠️ Ethical & Design Considerations

- Personas are built strictly from **public data**.
- The system is designed to provide **advisory insights**, not impersonation.
- Responses are framed as guidance inspired by public thought leadership, not factual claims or endorsements.

---

## 🚀 Learning Outcomes

Through building PersonaAI, I am exploring:
- Persona-based prompt engineering
- Knowledge grounding using public data sources
- Contextual response generation
- Designing AI systems that balance inspiration, accuracy, and ethical responsibility

PersonaAI aims to bridge the gap between inspiration and action by turning static content into **interactive, meaningful conversations**.
